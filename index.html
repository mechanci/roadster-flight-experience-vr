Flight Test

What would a thruster-assisted car flight feel like from the driver's seat, and is it ready for everyday use?

A seated ride for Meta Quest 3. Pick one of two flights, a ramp drop or a hop over a row of cars, then see what daily use would require. About a minute per ride.

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and tap Enter VR.

Before you ride

Sit down. The ride has fast acceleration, free-fall and (in Intense mode) a full flip, which can cause motion sickness. Start with Gentle. Squeeze a grip button at any time to stop, or take the headset off.

Not recommended if you are prone to motion sickness, seizures or have a heart condition.

About this project

Unofficial fan concept, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Tesla or SpaceX. The cabin is invented; the real Roadster interior has not been shown. Figures such as the 3.3 g peak come from press reports of the October 2026 unveil, and the ramp sequence follows a public reconstruction by Bart Blockmans (bartblockmans.com); none of it has been verified against Tesla data. VR cannot produce real g-force: this is a simulation of the feel, not a measurement.
